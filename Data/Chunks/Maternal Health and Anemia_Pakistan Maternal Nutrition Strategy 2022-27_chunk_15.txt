The WHO’s Preconception Care Policy Brief for Positive Impact on Maternal and Child Health Outcomes provides a foundation for implementing a package of promotive, preventive and curative health interventions during the preconception period.14 These have been shown to be effective in improving maternal and child health. The brief includes a nutrition section, which focuses on screening, monitoring, micronutrient supplementation and counselling.
