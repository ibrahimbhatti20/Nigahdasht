The PDHS 2017-18 indicates a decline in under-öve mortality to 74 per 1000 live births, from 89 in the PDHS 2006/07, both for the 3-year period preceding the survey. The under-5 mortality in PSLM 2018/19 was even lower (59 per 1,000 live births). The UN IGME considered this an underestimate of their estimated under-5 mortality in 2019 at 67 per 1,000 live births (with an average annual rate of reduction of 2.9% per year. These estimates imply an average annual rate of decrease of 8% per year to reach the 2030 target of 25 per 1000 live births, almost three times faster than the rate of decline in the last decade. Causes of under-öve deaths Surveillance for congenital anomalies has not yet been initiated at scale in Pakistan. The causes and patterns need to be understood for a focused action plan. Congenital anomalies can cause miscarriage, stillbirth, and for those who survive, lifelong physical and psychological diﬃculties.
