According to UNDP’s Human Development Report 2019, as discussed above, Pakistan’s ranks 152 of 189 countries, ranking low both on the Gender Development Index (GDI) with an index value of 0.747 (0.742 in 2016) and the Gender Inequality Index (GII), with a value 0.547 (0.546 in 2016). These results reflect entrenched gender inequalities in Pakistan, and only marginal improvements on key gender-related indicators in recent few years. Pakistan’s GII ranking has worsened, falling to 136 in 2019, from 133 in 2017. Pakistan still has low GDI and GII compared to other countries in the region.
