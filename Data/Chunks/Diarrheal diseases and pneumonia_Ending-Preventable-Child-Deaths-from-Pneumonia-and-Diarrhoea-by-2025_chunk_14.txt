Its goals are to mobilize political leadership, achieve consensus on a global roadmap highlighting innovative and proven strategies to accelerate reductions in child mortality, and drive sustained collective action and mutual accountability. It will achieve these goals through evidence-based country plans, transparency and mutual accountability, and global communication and social mobilization. • The 2012 Declaration on Scaling up Treatment of Diarrhea and Pneumonia (14) from donors, industry and nongovernmental organizations (NGOs) calls on all highburden countries and the international community to provide the resources, political will and focused action needed for ending preventable childhood pneumonia and diarrhoea deaths. • The World Health Assembly Resolution 63.24 (16), endorsed in 2010, encourages all countries to implement the actions outlined in the GAPP as a national priority.
