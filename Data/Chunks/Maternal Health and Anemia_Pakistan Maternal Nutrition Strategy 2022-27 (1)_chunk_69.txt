Provincial implementation Punjab has already established a provincial chapter of the Maternal, Infant, Young Child and Adolescent Nutrition Technical Advisory Group, while Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan will also set up MIYCAN TAG chapters of their own. These platforms will provide overall guidance and oversight. Nutrition programmes within provincial Departments of Health and other relevant departments will be responsible for the implementation of the Maternal Nutrition Strategy, in line with the commitments outlined in their respective provincial implementation plans. As with their federal counterparts, provincial Planning and Development Departments and the provincial chapters of the SUN initiative will support the coordination, resource mobilization, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of nutrition-sensitive interventions. The responsibilities of provincial government departments and authorities listed in Table 14 should be coordinated with the actions listed in the Pakistan Adolescent Nutrition Strategy, where applicable.
