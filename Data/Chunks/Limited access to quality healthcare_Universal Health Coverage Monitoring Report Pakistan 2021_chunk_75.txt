A disaggregation of budget allocations by purpose shows a concerning trend, whereby a disproportionately higher allocation has been reserved for hospital expenses, a significant proportion is devoted to health administration, and public health services have the least share of budget allocation. Punjab had the highest allocation of current expenditures for health, and spent 78 percent of its total budget on hospital services; while Balochistan had the lowest allocation for current health expenditures, and spent only 31 percent on hospital services.
