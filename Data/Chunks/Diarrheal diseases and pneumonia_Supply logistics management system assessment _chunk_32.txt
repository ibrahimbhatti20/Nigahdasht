SUPPLY / LOGISTICS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM ASSESSMENT 47 4.2.8.1 Infrastructure of warehouses The medicinal warehouses have been assessed to provide the baseline information of the storage conditions. Purpose built stores were rare, mostly, rooms were allotted as storage sites. Capacity of store rooms, for the health facilities were not enough to store the medicines as per recommended procedures. The structure of medicines store of district Kashmore Sindh and Rajanpur were not suitable to store the medicines. EDO health, LHW program of Kashmore have arranged a temporary store while Rajanpur is still using the same store.
