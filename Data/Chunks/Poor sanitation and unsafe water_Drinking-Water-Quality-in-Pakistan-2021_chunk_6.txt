This program was continued as a regular activity with monitoring frequency of five years. Following this, with addition of two more cities, it was extended to total 26 cities of Pakistan in 2015-16. This monitoring phase revealed that only 31% of the monitored water sources were safe for drinking purpose. The current water quality monitoring of 2020 is the national program implemented in 29 cities of four provinces, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan.
