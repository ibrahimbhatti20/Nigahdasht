Mothers with one or more risk factors are likely to have higher child mortality. The probability of children dying in infancy is much greater among children born to mothers who are too young (under age 18) or too old (over age 34), children born after a short birth interval (less than 24 months after the preceding birth), and children born to mothers of high parity (more than 3 children). The risk is elevated when a child is born to a mother who has a combination of these risk characteristics.
