Adult and Maternal Mortality Maternal Mortality The 2019 PMMS asked households about deaths among ever-married women age 15-49 in the 3 years before the survey. Households reporting a female death conducted a verbal autopsy interview to determine the cause of death. Maternal mortality includes deaths of ever-married women during pregnancy, delivery, and 42 days after delivery or end of pregnancy excluding deaths that were due to accidents or violence. The 2019 PMMS maternal mortality ratio (MMR) was estimated directly from maternal deaths identified in the verbal autopsy interviews of next of kin of deceased women. The MMR for Pakistan (excluding AJK and GB) is 186 deaths per 100,000 live births for the 3-year period before the survey. The confidence interval for the MMR ranges from 138 to 234 deaths per 100,000 live births. Regionally, there are differences in the MMR point estimates but the confidence intervals overlap, indicating that the differences are not statistically significant. MMR has declined from 276 in 2006-07. This evidence of a decline does not take account of statistical uncertainty in the estimates from the two surveys.
