The module also includes a field visit where participants will practice assessing and admitting at-risk mothers and infants under 6 months as pairs and children 6–59 months with SAM without medical complications to outpatient care. The field visit will also enable participants to practice managing infants and children in an outpatient care follow-on session. Participants will also have the opportunity during this field visit to practice the skills covered in Module 2. Defining and Measuring Acute Malnutrition.
