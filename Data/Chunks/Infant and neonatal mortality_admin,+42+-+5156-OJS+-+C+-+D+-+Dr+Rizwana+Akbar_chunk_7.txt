CONCLUSION In our study, it is concluded that not only the booked mothers in the selected military hospitals had better outcomes in terms of neonatal survival and health but neonatal health was strongly correlated with the number of antenatal visits of the mother. Neonatal morbidity and mortality can thus be prevented by increasing the utilization of existing antenatal care services, resulting in improved maternal, neonatal and perinatal health.
