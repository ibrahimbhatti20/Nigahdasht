First stage is a systematic spatial sample using triangular irregular network rather than a grid to identify communities to sample. The second stage is active and adaptive case finding to find all or nearly all SAM cases within the communities selected. Results S3M provides the following outputs: • Coverage map similar to that of CSAS • Overall estimate of coverage • Ranked barriers Figure 15 shows a map of coverage in a MoHdelivered CMAM programme in Niger produced using the S3M method.
