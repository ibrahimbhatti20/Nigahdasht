Policy transformation is a complex and painstaking process that requires clear-cut, precise and well-timed interplay of a multitude of factors. These factors, both intrinsic like political will and commitment, and extrinsic like broader policy environment should all work coherently to complete the causal chain of policy change, policy translation and then its implementation and ultimately, knowledge management of the impact.
