Livestock medication, more precisely veterinary services, seems to be a matter of concern as more than half of the households reported ‘inadequate’ (available but not sufficient) availability of the such services for their livestock. While another 25 percent of households informed that no such services are available at all for their livestock in their areas. Livestock related medication was found to be more a problem in TSD Bannu, North and South Waziristan Districts where 61 percent, 41 percent and 31 percent and households respectively reported ‘no availability at all’ of livestock related medication in their areas.
