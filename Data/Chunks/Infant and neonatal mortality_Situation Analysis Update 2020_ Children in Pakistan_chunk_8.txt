Open defecation remains fairly widespread and practiced by 10 percent of households in Pakistan, largely practiced by poor in rural areas. WASH and climate change are inextricably linked. Disasters can destroy water supplies and toilet, or leaving behind contaminated water and putting the lives of millions of children at risk. Every child has an equitable chance in life: Gender issues cut across all thematic areas and gender disparities are pronounced for children in Pakistan. The country ranks low both on the Gender Development Index (GDI) and the Gender Inequality Index GII), although its GDI and GII values have improved marginally in recent years.
