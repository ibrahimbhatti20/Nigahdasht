The objectives of eMTCT are to: • Keep the momentum on eMTCT as a priority component of global efforts to reaching MDGs 4, 5 and 6 • Provide a framework to set goals and targets, track progress and monitor performance at global, national and subnational levels • Foster prioritization of evidence-informed solutions to overcoming bottlenecks to progress • Mobilize and leverage partners and resources for country level action The Global plan provides an operational framework from which global partners, national governments, policy-makers, programme managers and implementing partners can draw to develop their own eMTCT goals and roadmap.
