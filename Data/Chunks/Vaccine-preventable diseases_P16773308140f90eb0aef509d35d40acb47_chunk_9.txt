During the NISP implementation period, which started in 2017/18, all provinces documented significant improvements in immunization coverage. Over the 28 years preceding NISP, i.e., between 1990 and 2018, Balochistan increased the proportion of fully immunized children by 62%, KP by 45%, Punjab by 107%, and Sindh by 93%. By contrast, during the NISP implementation, in only three years between 2017/18 and 2020, Balochistan improved coverage by 31%, KP by 25%, Punjab by 13%, and Sindh by 25%. This points to the huge contribution made by NISP. Despite the progress summarized in this section, a relatively large proportion of children remain unvaccinated. In Balochistan, in 2020, one in every three children aged 12–23 months was not vaccinated; compared to roughly one in 10 in KP (11.4%) or Sindh (7.2%); whereas in Punjab only 0.7% of children were not vaccinated in 2020.
