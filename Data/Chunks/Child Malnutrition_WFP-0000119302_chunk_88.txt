Therefore, CFSLA also looked into the main risks, children are prone to in the Merged Areas. Majority (66%) households reported as such ‘no risk’ to children, whereas 24.6 percent highlighted environmental risks such as accidents, open pit latrines, riversides, dangerous animals, flood water, electric wires etc. at home and outside. Among the Districts, 57 percent households in South Waziristan mentioned the highest prevalence of such environmental risks to children, followed by TSD Tank (54.5%) and North Waziristan (34.1%).
