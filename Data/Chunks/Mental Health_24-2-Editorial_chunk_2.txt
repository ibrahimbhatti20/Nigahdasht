A positive political attitude should be created by highlighting the importance of mental health on all concerned forums especially using media. This will lead to an environment which is based on understanding and trust, helpful in the formulation and implementation of mental health policy. It is needless to say that all the main sectors performing their specific roles and responsibilities can make any 1 policy a success . The final goal, thus, is the development of a mental health policy that can deliver comprehensive mental health care by integrating it with primary care but this will need competence, perseverance and motivational enthusiasm as it may take one to two years to develop and five to ten years, may be more, to 1 implement a policy .
