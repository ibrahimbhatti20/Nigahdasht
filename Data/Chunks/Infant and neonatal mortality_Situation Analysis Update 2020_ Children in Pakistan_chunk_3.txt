The SitAn Update also emphasizes the impact of the global COVID-19 pandemic on the realisation of child rights’ outcomes for children in Pakistan. The contextual framework used for the analysis of developments in the past three years is based on the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) that are especially relevant for child rights. The analysis follows a rights-based, equity-focused approach, building on the 2017 Situation Analysis of Children in Pakistan.
