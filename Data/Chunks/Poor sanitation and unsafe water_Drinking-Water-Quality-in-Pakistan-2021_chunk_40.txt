The monitoring outcomes of the year 2020 with 51% of unsafe water sources was comparatively lowest than the previous monitoring years (2002-06, 2010-15). The major problems, identified in province, were Bacterial contaminants, Arsenic, Fluorides, TDS and Nitrates present in the drinking water. From 2002 to 2006, it ranged from 74% to 78%, which decreased to 35% in 2010. This level of safe water supplies gradually decreased in 2015 and 2020. Iron is main contaminant in the province during monitoring year 2015 and 2020. It may be due to nearby high amount of parent material having iron rich minerals.
