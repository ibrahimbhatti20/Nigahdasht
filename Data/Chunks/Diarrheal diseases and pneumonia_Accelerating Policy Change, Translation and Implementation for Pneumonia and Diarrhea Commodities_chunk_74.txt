Diarrhea in children under 05 years of age was done through review of prescriptions of children visiting surveyed healthcare facilities. A total of 480 prescriptions were recorded with 10 from each of 48 surveyed facilities, and 473 of which turned out to be containing relevant information. Out of these prescriptions, 45.7% were Pneumonia patients and 54.3% were suffering from Diarrhea. Among these children, 52.6% were boys and 47.4% were girls, with variation more pronounced among Diarrhea patients as 54.5% male children were suffering from Diarrhea as compared to 50.5% male children suffering from Pneumonia.
