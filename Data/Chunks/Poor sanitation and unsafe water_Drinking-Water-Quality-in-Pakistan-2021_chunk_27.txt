In 2015, four sampling locations were non-functional. To manage this situation, alternative four sites were chosen for samples collection in 2020. Microbiologically all samples were found safe. However, 55% of the sources contained Iron (Fe) beyond the NDWQS level of 0.03 mg/l (Table 19).
