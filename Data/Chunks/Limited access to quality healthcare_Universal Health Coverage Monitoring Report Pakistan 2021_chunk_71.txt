The devastating earthquake of October 2005 and floods of 2010 took thousands of precious lives and rendered millions homeless. As a result of this lesson learning, the National Disaster Management Act was enacted in the country during 2010. Accordingly, a National Action Plan to strengthen the existing systems was developed, setting strategic priorities to achieve a robust health system in Pakistan by aligning with the National Disaster Management Plan and National DRR Policy.
