Training Guide for Community-Based Management of Acute Malnutrition (CMAM), 2018 Version 162 Module 8 FIELD PRACTICE LEARNING OBJECTIVE 3: REVIEW THE SYSTEM FOR RECORDING RUTF DISTRIBUTION AND STOCK LEVELS HANDS-ON REVIEW AT SITE: REVIEW WITH STAFF SYSTEM FOR RECORDING RUTF DISTRIBUTED, IN STOCK AND PROCUREMENT • With staff, review RUTF: distribution, in-stock and procurement. • Go over a supervision checklist within small groups. • Fill out (parts of) a supervision checklist based on observed activities linked to supplies at outpatient care.
