Though neonatal mortality stagnated at 55 deaths per 1,000 live births for nearly a decade between 2006 and 2013, it has declined to 42 deaths per 1,000 live births in the most recent five-year period.⁴ Vaccination coverage Full vaccination coverage has improved over the past five years from 54 per cent to 66 per cent, meaning that two-thirds of Pakistani children are now fully immunized. However, 4 per cent of children aged 12-23 months have not received any of the six basic vaccinations — a slight reduction in the past five years, but a remaining challenge.
