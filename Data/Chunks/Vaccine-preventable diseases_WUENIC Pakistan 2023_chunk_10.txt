Low drop-out rates indicate high retention of children in immunization programmes. This chart shows trends in dropout rates between DTP1 and DTP3, and DTP1 and MCV1. In 2023, 9% of children who received DTP1 did not receive DTP3 (left), and 11% of children who received DTP1 did not receive MCV1 (right). The medium DTP drop-out rates imply moderate ability to provide a complete series of vaccines early in life. The high DTP-MCV dropout rates imply poor retention in immunization programmes and ability to provide a full course of vaccines in infancy (up to one year). In 2023, Pakistan DTP drop-out was higher and DTP-MCV dropout was higher than global dropout rates, respectively.
