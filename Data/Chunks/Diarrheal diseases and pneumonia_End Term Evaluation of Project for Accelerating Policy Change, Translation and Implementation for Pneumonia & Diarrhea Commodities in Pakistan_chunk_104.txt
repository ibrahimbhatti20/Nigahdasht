In contrast to a common belief that Diarrhea is the disease of summers, evaluation findings revealed high caseload of Diarrhea in winters with 94.8 mean number of cases per facility as compared to 72.8 mean number of cases of Pneumonia in the surveyed facilities. As compared to findings of PDHS 2017-18, this caseload depicted improvement in care seeking behaviors of the communities for U5 Pneumonia and Diarrhea, with more children being taken to the facility for management.
