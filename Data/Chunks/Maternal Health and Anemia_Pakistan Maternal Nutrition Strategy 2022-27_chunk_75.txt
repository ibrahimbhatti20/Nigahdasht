Population surveys in Pakistan – the National Nutrition Survey, the Pakistan Demographic and Health Survey and Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys (MICS) – already include indicators related to maternal nutrition. However, advocacy may be needed to ensure the inclusion of additional indicators. Phase 8 of the Demographic and Health Survey (DHS) has added new indicators on maternal nutrition that could be recommended for inclusion in the Pakistan Demographic and Health Survey.
