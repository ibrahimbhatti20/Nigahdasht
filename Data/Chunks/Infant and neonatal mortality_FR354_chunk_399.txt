Similarly, another 11% said that they were embarrassed about seeking help (Table 16.19). 16.8.4 Reasons for Not Seeking Help The survey also assessed reasons why women who experienced violence did not seek help. Overall, 24% of women who had experienced physical or sexual violence did not think that the violence was serious enough for them to seek help. Fifteen percent of women were embarrassed or ashamed to seek help, while 12% felt it would bring a bad name to their family (Table 16.20). Nine percent feared threats, consequences, or more violence. Even though only 2% of women reported that they would not be believed or would instead be blamed for the violence themselves, this remains an important reason why women keep silent about the violence they face.
