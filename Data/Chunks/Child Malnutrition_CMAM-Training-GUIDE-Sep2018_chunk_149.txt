Explain to participants that they are going to practice compiling information for site tally sheets from outpatient care treatment cards, and for a site report from the site tally sheet. Refer participants to Exercise 8.2 Completing Site Tally Sheet. Ask them to form pairs and read the instructions. Answer any questions and, while they are completing the site tally sheet and site report, circulate among the groups to check on their progress. Check answers against the answer key below, and answer any questions. Emphasize to participants that these reports can take many forms and that it is essential to determine and coordinate with reporting systems used in the country and district they are working in.
