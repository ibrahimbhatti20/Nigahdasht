EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION (ECE)/PRE-PRIMARY EDUCATION Pre-primary level in Pakistan largely consist of katchi classes which are not necessarily quality ECE classrooms. In Pakistan, many children are enrolled at pre-primary levels, but few schools offer an ECE program. Most of these children do not have any specific resources allocated to them e.g. teacher, room or supplies. Although this is an informal space, in practice it serves as an entry class for public sector primary schools.
