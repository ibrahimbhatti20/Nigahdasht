Agriculture Supports Required by Households In order to get an idea on the agriculture related needs of the households involved in farming activities, CFLSA captured information on their priority needs. Overall, about 40 percent of the households reported quality seeds as their priority need followed by 32 percent fertiliser and 19 percent introduction of new irrigation system as their most pressing. In the Merged Areas, farmers have limited access to quality inputs. This limited access can be attributed to the non-availability of quality inputs at the local level as well as the low purchasing power of the farmers. Thus, the use of inferior quality of agriculture inputs leads to poor agricultural production. Other priority needs of the farming households included repair and/or improvements to the current irrigation system, agricultural tools, repairs of tube wells, and the extension of agriculture services.
