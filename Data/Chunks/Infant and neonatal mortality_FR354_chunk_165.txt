Similarly, men in the lowest (35%) and second wealth quintile (30%) are more likely to agree with this statement than men in the fourth (25%) or highest (23%) wealth quintiles. ▪ The highest proportion of men who agree with the statement that contraception is women’s business are from Balochistan (40%). Interestingly, the lowest percentage of men who believe that contraception may make women more promiscuous is also in Balochistan (1%). ▪ The highest proportion of men who believe that contraception makes women more promiscuous was in Punjab (23%), but there was a large difference there between urban men (13%) and rural men (30%).
