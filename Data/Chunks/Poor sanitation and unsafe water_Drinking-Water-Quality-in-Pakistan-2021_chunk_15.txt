Care was taken to keep the bottle well above the bed of the stream to avoid unwanted bed material going into the sample. 8 2.3.6 Spring Water Water samples were collected directly from the spring in sterilized sampling bottles for microbiology and bottles used with or without preservatives for other water quality parameters. 2.3.7 Dam, River and Lake It is difficult to obtain a truly representative sample when collecting surface water samples. Sampling point was selected carefully (near to bank in case of river) to avoid any kind of debris in the water. Considerable variations like seasonal stratification, runoff, rainfall and wind were also documented while collecting water sample especially from lake.
