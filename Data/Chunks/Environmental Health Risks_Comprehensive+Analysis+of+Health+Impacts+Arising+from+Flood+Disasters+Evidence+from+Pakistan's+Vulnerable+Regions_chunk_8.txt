Catastrophic flooding has profound effects on both physical and mental health, often through the proliferation of bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens. Such diseases can encompass diarrhea, cholera, dengue fever, and hepatitis E. Stagnant floodwaters create optimal environments for mosquitoes and other disease vectors. Beyond infectious ailments, the ramifications of flooding also encompass long-term health issues, including chronic respiratory conditions, anxiety, and depression. Displaced populations are especially vulnerable, as they often lack access to clean water, sanitation, and healthcare services. Specific demographics, including the elderly, pregnant women, children, and those with pre-existing health concerns, exhibit heightened susceptibility.
