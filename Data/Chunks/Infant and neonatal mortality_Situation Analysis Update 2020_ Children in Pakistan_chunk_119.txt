Cultural norms in Pakistan perpetuate women’s marginalization, causing women to have less access and control over health care resources, and to be disadvantaged in decision-making on their own health. As a result, women in Pakistan tend to have poor health outcomes. These range from a high maternal mortality ratio, to high rates of malnutrition, communicable and non-communicable diseases.
