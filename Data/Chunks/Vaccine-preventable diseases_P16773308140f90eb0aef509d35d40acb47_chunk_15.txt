Training and use of information technology can enhance the capacity of the vaccinators and EPI teams. · Ensuring sustainable financing for immunization: One of the key achievements under NISP is the transfer of immunization-related budget from development partner financing to the government's share. In addition, dedicated accounting codes have been created to track immunization-related budget and expenditure. This helps the government in projecting total requirements and estimating needs. Given the changing burden of diseases, it is critical to ensure that the budgetary needs for immunization are adequately financed and the program is not hampered due to financial constraints. · Foster partnership: Partnership with the private sector can improve the efficacy of the EPI program. It can also help in promoting vaccine uptake and reaching out to areas where there are limited vaccination sites. There are good practices documented by other countries in the region that Pakistan can explore. · Explore innovative approaches for increasing demand for vaccination: conditional or coresponsibility cash transfers (CCT) have proven to be effective in improving HNP outcomes, particularly creating demand for immunization, in many countries in Asia and Africa. Under a CCT, cash is given to targeted beneficiaries upon fulfillment of conditions or coresponsibilities designed to promote uptake of selected services, such as vaccination. This is being piloted under NISP and can be rolled out if proven successful in the context of Pakistan. 4. Conclusions and way forward 28 16
