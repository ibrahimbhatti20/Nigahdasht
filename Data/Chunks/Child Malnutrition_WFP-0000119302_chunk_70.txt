Coping Strategy Index/Consumption Based Coping Mechanisms Coping Strategy Index (CSI) is a proxy indicator used to assess the hardship faced by households. The CSI measures the frequency and severity of the behaviours of households display when they face food shortages. The food-based coping strategies, also called Reduced CSI (r-CSI) include the behaviours adopted by households when they have difficulties meeting their food needs. It is calculated using standard food consumption-based strategies and severity weighting .
