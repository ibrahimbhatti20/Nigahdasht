Author contributions SKM and JD conceptualised this study idea; SKM, LGP, DGB, SS, SA, MH, JD, and ZAB played a role in the study’s design, planning and implementation; AB translated verbal autopsy responses; JD, SKM, AK, and SA interpreted verbal autopsies. JD, LGF and DSF analysed the data. JD wrote the first draft of the manuscript and led revisions. All authors read, contributed edits and approved the final version of the manuscript. Disclosure statement The authors report no conflicts of interest. Ethics The study was approved by the Research Ethics Board at The Hospital for Sick Children (REB No. 100,042,963) and the National Bioethics Committee, Pakistan (No.4-87/13/NBC133/RDC/2629) and was registered with Clinicaltrials.gov (NCT02130856). Baby Hero (https://babyhe.ro) further supported the study. SKM received support from the Canadian Child Health Clinician Scientist Program Career Development Award.
