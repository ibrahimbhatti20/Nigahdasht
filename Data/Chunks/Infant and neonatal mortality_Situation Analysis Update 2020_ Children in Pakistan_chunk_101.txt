The Gilgit-Baltistan Prohibition of Corporal Punishment Act 2015, the Prohibition of Corporal Punishment Act in 2017 in Islamabad Capital Territory, and the Sindh Prohibition of Corporal Punishment Act 2016 prohibit all forms of corporal punishment and the humiliating or degrading treatment of children in day care settings.169 The Islamabad High Court (IHC) has suspended, until further notice, section 89 of the Pakistan Penal Code (PPC) that permits the use of corporal punishment by parents, guardians and teachers “in good faith for the benefit [of a child]”.170  Sindh enacted the Sindh Prohibition of Employment of Children Act 2017, which establishes 15 as the minimum age for employment and 19 as the minimum age for employment in hazardous work.  The Federal Government enacted the Islamabad Capital Territory Child Protection Act 2018 to establish a minimum age for work and hazardous work. It outlines a list of hazardous work and rules the worst forms of child labour a criminal offence in the federal capital.  The Prevention of Trafficking in Persons Act 2018, enacted by the Federal Government, seeks to prevent and punish human trafficking, including the trafficking of women and children.  In 2019, the Senate passed the Child Marriage Restraint (Amendment) Bill which proposes that the legal minimum age of marriage in the country be set at 18 for both girls and boys.
