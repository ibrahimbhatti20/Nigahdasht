Page 1 2019 Pakistan Maternal Mortality Survey The 2019 Pakistan Maternal Mortality Survey (PMMS) is designed to provide data for monitoring maternal health in Pakistan. The 2019 PMMS is the first nationwide survey on maternal mortality in Pakistan, along with four Demographic and Health Surveys conducted in Pakistan since 199091. The objective of the survey was to provide reliable estimates of maternal health, morbidity, and mortality indicators that can be used by programme managers and policymakers to evaluate and improve existing programmes.
