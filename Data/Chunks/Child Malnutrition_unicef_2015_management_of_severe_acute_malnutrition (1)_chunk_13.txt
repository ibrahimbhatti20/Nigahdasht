Thus, the management of SAM needs to be scaled up through deliberate efforts to increase programme coverage for treatment of children. Successful efforts address both horizontal dimensions (systems) and vertical dimensions (geographic coverage). This combined approach represents the only sustainable way to raise the impact of SAM treatment and develop highquality programmes.
