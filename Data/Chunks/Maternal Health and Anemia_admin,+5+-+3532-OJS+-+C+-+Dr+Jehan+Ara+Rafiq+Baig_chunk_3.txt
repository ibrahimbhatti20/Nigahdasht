DISCUSSION Maternal anemia is a common problem encountered by gynecologists and obstetricians worldwide, especially in developing countries. Number of studies has found an association between anemia in pregnancy and adverse maternal and neonatal outcomes9. Neonatal adverse outcomes associated with maternal anemia include fetal anemia, stillbirth, low and very low birth weight babies, poor APGAR score, small for gestational age, birth asphyxia, and preterm delivery10.
