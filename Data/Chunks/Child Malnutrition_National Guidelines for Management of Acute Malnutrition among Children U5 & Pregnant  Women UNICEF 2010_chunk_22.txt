In an emergency it can also be established anywhere where: ▪ Children can stay for 24 hours a day ▪ The appropriately skilled personnel are available ▪ Clean water is available ▪ Milk preparation area available (for boiling water) The environment must be clean. Clean bedding and mosquito nets (in malarial areas) must be provided. Plenty of clean blankets are also required. WHEN: Children stay in in-patient care until the medical complications are treated and they have regained appetite. On average this is 4-7 days.
