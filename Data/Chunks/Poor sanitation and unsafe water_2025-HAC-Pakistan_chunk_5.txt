For adolescent girls, malnutrition may delay puberty, hinder development and lead to reproductive health issues. Malnourished mothers also face higher risks of poor health and pregnancy complications. If the appeal is fully supported, UNICEF will be able to reach 14 per cent31 of the children in Pakistan in critical need of humanitarian support and access to essential services. In keeping with Grand Bargain commitments, UNICEF seeks multi-year and flexible funding for the multifaceted humanitarian needs faced by children and families in Pakistan. Without adequate and timely funding, UNICEF and its partners will be unable to address children's critical needs for health services, nutrition support, prevention of school drop-out and protection from violence, exploitation and abuse.
