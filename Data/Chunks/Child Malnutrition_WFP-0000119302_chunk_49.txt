Access to food markets is relatively better in the Tribal Sub-Divisions, as for food the households in Tribal Sub-Divisions are dependent on markets in the adjacent settled Districts. Whereas in the rest of the Merged Districts, there are fewer markets which are relatively less dynamic. The main problems reported by the households while accessing food markets include; access roads are damaged (33%) and markets are far away (30%), another 11 percent and 7 percent mentioned ‘high transportation cost’ and transport often not available as the main problem while accessing the nearest food markets.
