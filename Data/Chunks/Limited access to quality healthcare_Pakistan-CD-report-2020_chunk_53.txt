DHIS reports compared for 2019 and 2020 clearly show the declining trends of consultations at government facilities. During April – May – June the number of children with ARI seen in facilities was down by 41% compared to the preceding year. The corresponding number of decrease in diarrhoea consultations was 39.7%.
