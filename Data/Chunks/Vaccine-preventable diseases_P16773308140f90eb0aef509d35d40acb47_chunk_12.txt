With respect to coverage of DTP3 and measles (first dose) vaccination, Pakistan had the second lowest coverage in the South Asia region in 2020 (see Figure 9). In terms of incidence of VPDs, Pakistan had the second highest incidence of both diphtheria and polio in South Asia in 2020. With respect to measles, the incidence in Pakistan was the fourth highest (see Figure 10). Pakistan and Afghanistan are the only two countries in the world where polio is still prevalent.
