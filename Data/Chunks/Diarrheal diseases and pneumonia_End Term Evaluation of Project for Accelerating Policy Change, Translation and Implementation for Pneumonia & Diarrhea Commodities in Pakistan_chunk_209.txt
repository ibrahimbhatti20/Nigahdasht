You can begin by saying these words to the group of participants: ‘As you know pneumonia and diarrhea are common causes of deaths among children under age 5. We are conducting an evaluation to learn about of services being provided by you to prevent and control these diseases among children and would like to know your views in this regard. The guideline contains multiple questions and each question is followed by a set of probes. Please let participants initiate the discussion and use probes only when certain concerns are not addressed by the group.
