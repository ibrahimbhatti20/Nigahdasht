Recommendation 8: Enhancing community awareness on childhood illnesses – A structured mass awareness campaign on childhood illness of the community, should be initiated, with support of development partners, through print, electronic and social media. Specific actions to raise community awareness include the following: • Use of print, electronic and social media to promote updated commodities for their ease of use, effectiveness and convenience. • Targeting populations with high burden of U5 Pneumonia and Diarrhea to raise positive awareness while keeping in view socio-economic and gender inequalities.
