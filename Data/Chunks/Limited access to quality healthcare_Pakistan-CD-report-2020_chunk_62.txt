Developing inter-sectoral linkages for addressing the underlying risk factors of low female social status, malnutrition, air pollution and inadequate WASH behaviours. Pakistan has indeed made some positive strides in improving maternal, newborn and child health of its people in the last decades. Challenges of the health system, and equity and high unmet need for family planning need urgent attention to accelerate progress in the next decade. This calls for fundamental health sector reforms to deliver a high-priority eﬀective RMNCAH package of quality services to the women and children who need it most.
