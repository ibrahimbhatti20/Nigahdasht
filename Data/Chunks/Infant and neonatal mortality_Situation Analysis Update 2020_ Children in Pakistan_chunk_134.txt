INVESTMENT UNDER ANNUAL DEVELOPMENT PLANS FOR 2019–2020 Donor funds allocated to projects in the current federal and provincial ADP budgets (2019–20) total PKR 51 billion in the areas on which UNICEF works. Most foreign assistance targets social protection followed by WASH and education. This suggests governments’ interest in seeking development partner investments in these areas.
