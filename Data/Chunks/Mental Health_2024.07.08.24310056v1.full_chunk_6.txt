Results In total 20833 households were approached as per study procedure in the four provinces of Pakistan, and 17,773 eligible participants were interviewed (response rate, 86.2%). The reasons for non-response were a locked house (n=628), non-residential building (n=144), no adult above 18 years was available at home (n=369), local area or residential security staff did not allow the team to work (n=13), refusal at door (n=1329), house doors were not opened (n=339), no one fulfilling the inclusion criteria was present at home (n=54).
