Pakistan with its GDP per capita of US$4,889 had a DTP3 coverage rate of 75% compared to Cambodia with GDP per capita of US$4,574 and 92% DTP3 coverage, Nepal US$4,120 and 93% coverage, Bangladesh US$4,955 and 98%, or Myanmar US$5,297 and 90%. In addition, there is a need to further improve efficiency of the EPI program.
