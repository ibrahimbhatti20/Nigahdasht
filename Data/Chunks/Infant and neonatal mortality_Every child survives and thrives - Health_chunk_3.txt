We also support the Government of Pakistan to reach its target to provide integrated health services for newborns and children under the age of one in the most disadvantaged communities, in line with its national and global commitments. HOW UNICEF WILL HELP ACHIEVE RESULTS If the policies, standards, guidelines and action plans on neonatal and child survival are planned and implemented with evidencebased data and based on verification from provinces, especially data from disadvantaged communities… If political support and commitment are the driving forces behind setting accountability measures for improving health service delivery and demand creation… If a health communication and promotion structure and system is operationalised to strengthen social mobilization and behaviour change communication to achieve our targets for health service delivery and demand creation… If there is practical coordination and synergy between relevant sectors (Health, Polio, WASH and Nutrition) to improve access and utilization of basic services in disadvantaged communities… If regular monitoring and evaluation is a standard practice within the health programme to check progress, periodically improve the programme design, and measure the interventions’ impact for knowledge sharing, scale-up and replication… …Then the most disadvantaged newborns and children will be benefiting from integrated newborn and child health interventions and healthy behaviours.
