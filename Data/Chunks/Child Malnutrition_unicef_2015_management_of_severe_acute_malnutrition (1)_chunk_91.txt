The World Bank has thus used the figure of around $41 per disability-adjusted life year (DALY) averted for inclusion of CMAM in its analysis of the cost of scaled-up nutrition (Horton et al. 201098). Despite this progress, there has been no systematic initiative to code or capture programme spending for management of SAM in UNICEF, unlike the system to capture spending on RUTF. This is an important issue given that UNICEF and donors need to be able to predict, monitor and account for costs, both for continued support and to encourage governments to take a larger role in funding.
