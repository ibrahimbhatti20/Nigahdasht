Implementation of the NCAP will address air quality issues in Pakistan and minimize their associated adverse impacts, including those on health, economy and development, and climate change and the environment Assuming a growth in the GDP rate of 2.77% per year9, the GDP of Pakistan can be expected to reach USD 480.11 billion in 20 years. Based on welfare losses at 5.88% of GDP and forgone labor output losses at 0.81% of GDP10, a health benefit of USD 24.56 billion per annum could be achieved through implementation of the Policy in terms of avoided health impacts Research shows that an improved national average lifespan of 2 years11 is expected as the Policy prioritizes reduction of PM2.5. At a 2% annual growth rate, the policy interventions are projected to reduce the annual number of deaths by nearly 129,500. An additional economic benefit of implementation of the NCAP would be improved crop growth rates due to elimination of air pollutants including ozone pollution. If NCAP is implemented an additional economic benefit would be improved crop growth rates and reduced air pollutants including ozone pollution. Assuming that implementation of the Policy would result in elimination of all pollutant sources evenly12, and that the presence of ozone is linearly proportional to the crop losses of 0.76% of GDP13, a total benefit of USD 2.79 billion per annum can be expected in terms of avoided crop losses. The Policy will bridge the gaps in governance mechanism, coordination, institutional strengthening, building capacity and providing monitoring and compliance framework for air quality both at national and provincial levels.
