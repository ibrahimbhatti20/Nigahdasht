EXECUTIVE SUMMARY IMPROVING MATERNAL, NEWBORN AND CHILD HEALTH OUTCOMES IN A DECADE PAKISTAN REPORT 2020 3 Newborn mortality rate has fallen to 41 per 1,000 live births and still birth rate to 31 per 1,000 total births. Both neonatal mortality and stillbirth rates decreased slowly at an annual rate of reduction of only 2% in the last decade. Research showed major gaps in the quality of neonatal interventions. To fasttrack progress, attention is needed to ensure quality of care around the time of birth and to invest in small and sick newborn care.
