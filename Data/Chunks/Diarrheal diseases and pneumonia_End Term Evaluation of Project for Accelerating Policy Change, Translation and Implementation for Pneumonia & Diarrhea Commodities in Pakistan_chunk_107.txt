In order to ascertain the overall trends of childhood mortality in Pakistan, estimates developed by the UN Inter-agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation (UNICEF, WHO, World Bank, UN DESA Population Division), corresponding to project years were analyzed to illustrate the change in childhood mortality in Pakistan with reference to project implementation timeframe of 2016 to 2020. As shown in figure 15, U5 mortality rates had reduced steadily in Pakistan. It was 73.8 deaths per 1,000 live births in 2016, which had reduced to 65.2 in 2020. There had been marked variations on the basis of gender of the child. For girls, it reduced from 69.0 deaths per 1,000 live births to 60.5 and for boys, it reduced from 78.4 to 69.6.
